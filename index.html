Install EventClock App

Install EventClock directly onto your phone or computer for instant full-screen access and live competition countdowns:

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Tap the Share button (⬆️) at the bottom or top of Safari.
2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

Android / Chrome / Edge

1. Tap the browser menu (⋮ or ⋯) in the top corner.
2. Tap Install App or Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Install to place EventClock on your home screen.
